Product Teardown

KreditBee — the KYC stack nobody fixed, just made skippable

A single-lender lending app's own product decisions reveal exactly where users give up — you just have to read the escape hatches they built.

§T1

Funnel, as it actually exists today

  1. Download & sign up
    Mobile number + OTP verification.
  2. Eligibility check
    PAN, DOB, monthly salary, PIN code — a soft check, no documents yet.
  3. KYC upload
    Aadhaar/Voter ID/Passport, address proof, marital status, selfie — several documents in one sitting.
  4. Income verification
    Bank statement upload — explicitly optional, can be skipped and completed later.
  5. Profile verification
    Backend review of submitted documents.
  6. Loan offer
    Amount + tenure selection, EMI shown instantly.
  7. Bank details
    Entered for disbursal.
  8. Disbursal
    Funds in ~10–15 minutes once verified.
§T2

Where the friction actually is

The single most telling detail in KreditBee's own flow is that income verification is explicitly optional and deferrable — suggesting the flow may be designed to reduce friction at that step rather than require completion upfront. The observable product decision suggests that the team may have prioritized reducing friction at this step rather than requiring completion upfront. The underlying reason for that decision cannot be established without primary research.

Friction point Steps 3–4, the KYC + income-verification stack. A first-time borrower has to produce and correctly photograph 3–4 separate documents in one sitting, on a phone — often mid-emergency, since KreditBee's own reviews mention "medical emergency" and "needed money fast" as real use contexts. That's exactly the moment someone has the least patience for a multi-step document flow.
§T3

The fix

Proposal Replace the static multi-document upload screen with a conversational, one-document-at-a-time AI agent flow: the agent asks for one document, gives real-time feedback if the photo is blurry, cropped, or the wrong document type — before the user moves on, instead of a generic "verification failed" screen days later — and tells the user plainly what's still needed and why.

This is close to exactly what Revrag's embedded, multilingual in-app AI agents are built to do for BFSI onboarding — turning a form into a guided conversation. I'm noting that directly rather than treating it as a coincidence: this teardown led me to the same problem their product exists to solve.

§T4

Metric that would prove it worked

Primary KYC completion rate within the first session — started KYC → fully submitted, same session, no drop to a later day. This targets the real failure mode: people abandoning mid-document and never coming back, not people rejecting the loan offer itself.
SecondaryRe-upload rate — how often a document gets rejected and needs resubmission. If the agent works, this should fall, since it catches bad photos before submission instead of after backend review.
