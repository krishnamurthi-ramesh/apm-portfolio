E-COMMERCE AI DISCOVERY TEARDOWN 2026 Analysis

Comet (E-commerce AI Search) — Teardown & Friction Analysis

Diagnosing drop-off friction between AI recommendation generation and Product Detail Page (PDP) conversion.

Author: Krishnamurthi
Product Focus: Comet AI "Finder" Flow
Primary Metric: Finder-Assisted Add-to-Cart Rate
Problem Statement

“Shoppers engage heavily in conversational discovery, but bounce when transitioning to checkout because size, fit, and aesthetic constraints get lost at the PDP boundary.”

Reverse-engineered Comet’s AI “Finder” discovery flow to diagnose drop-off friction between recommendation generation and PDP conversion.

IDENTIFIED FRICTION

Context Amnesia at Checkout

Users spend 2-3 minutes refining specific preferences (e.g. "oversized linen shirts for humid weather under $60"). When clicking through to the PDP, the page opens as a generic product view without the selected size or context, forcing the user to re-evaluate from scratch.

PROPOSED INTERVENTION

Session Memory & Context Carry-Over

Proposed a context-aware conversational memory solution to carry size/fit parameters across sessions to reduce cart abandonment, defining “Finder-assisted add-to-cart rate” as the primary validation metric.
