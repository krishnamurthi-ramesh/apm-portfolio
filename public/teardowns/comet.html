Comet (E-commerce AI Search): From “Which Sneaker?” to “Which Comet Is Me?”

Reverse-engineering Comet’s AI “Finder” discovery flow to eliminate conversational amnesia and drop-off friction between recommendation generation and Product Detail Page (PDP) conversion.

Author: Krishnamurthi (AI PM)
Product Focus: Comet AI “Finder” & Silhouette Finder 2.0
Primary Metric: Finder-Assisted Add-to-Cart Rate (+22% Target)
Core Friction Hypothesis

“Shoppers engage heavily in conversational discovery, but 42% bounce before reaching the PDP because size, fit, and lifestyle constraints get wiped at the Product Detail boundary.”

Comet offers multiple silhouettes (X Lows, Aeres, Aeon v2, ASTRA, Apex) with technical footwear attributes, but the cognitive translation from attribute → personal lifestyle need → product choice creates decision fatigue when stateless chat filters reset on click-through.

IDENTIFIED FRICTION

Stateless Amnesia at Checkout

Users spend 2–3 minutes refining specific preferences (e.g., “oversized comfort sneakers for long walks under ₹5,000”). When clicking through to the PDP, the page loads a generic product view without the discussed size or fit profile retained, forcing shoppers to re-filter from scratch.

PROPOSED INTERVENTION

Context-Aware Session Memory

Designed structured session memory persistence to carry size, fit preference, and style vectors directly into pre-selected PDP states and one-click add-to-cart triggers, targeting 0-reset fit retention and defining Finder-assisted add-to-cart rate as the North Star metric.

Solution Architecture: Silhouette Finder 2.0

A use-case-first discovery flow: asking 4 high-signal questions, then delivering ranked candidates with persistent context.

Q1 • Use Case

What will you use them for?

Everyday commute, Long walks & travel, Night out, or Style-first statement.

Q2 • Fit Preference

How do you like your fit?

Roomy & relaxed, Balanced standard, or Snug locked-in athletic feel.

Q3 • Aesthetic Style

What is your style profile?

Minimalist clean, Streetwear bold, Retro court, or Chunky runner.

Q4 • Priority Vector

What matters most to you?

All-day underfoot comfort, Lightweight agility, Bold styling, or Universal versatility.

PM Judgment Call: Recommendation vs. Discovery

Balancing prescriptive AI guidance against organic brand serendipity.

Overly rigid single-choice recommendations make Comet feel like a clinical utility catalog. Too little structure triggers choice paralysis. The PM resolution: never recommend just one shoe in isolation. Surface 2–3 ranked silhouettes with transparent attribute trade-offs carried directly into the PDP:

1. X LOWS — Best for: Street + everyday wear, Roomier fit.
2. AERES — Best for: Lightweight everyday wear, Narrower streamlined fit.
3. APEX — Best for: Maximum cushioning, Relaxed casual weekend styling.

Success Metrics & A/B Experiment

Validating conversion lift without deteriorating return and exchange rates.

Primary North Star

Finder-Assisted Add-to-Cart

Conversion rate from Silhouette Finder completion to PDP add-to-cart within the same browsing session (+22% target).

Secondary Lift

Zero-Repeat-Filter Sessions

Percentage of shoppers reaching checkout without manually resetting or retyping size/fit parameters.

Guardrail Metric

Return & Exchange Rate

30-day post-purchase return rate must remain flat or decrease, proving recommendations match physical fit.
