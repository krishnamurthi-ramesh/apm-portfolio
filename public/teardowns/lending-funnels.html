PART 01 • DIRECT LENDER TEARDOWN

KreditBee: The KYC Stack Nobody Fixed, Just Made Skippable

A single-lender app’s product escape hatches reveal precisely where first-time borrowers abandon checkout.

Observational Insight

The single most telling detail in KreditBee’s flow is that income verification was made explicitly optional and deferrable — a tactical escape hatch acknowledging mid-funnel abandonment at the 3–4 document camera upload wall.

The 8-Step Funnel Anatomy

01. Download & Sign Up Mobile number + OTP verification
02. Eligibility Check PAN, DOB, salary, PIN code (soft check, zero docs)
03. KYC Document Upload [CHOKE POINT] Aadhaar/PAN, address proof, selfie — high blur & drop-off
04. Income Verification [ESCAPE HATCH] Bank statement upload — explicitly made optional
05. Profile Verification Delayed asynchronous OCR & underwriting queue
06. Loan Offer Selection Amount + tenure selection with EMI breakdown
07. Bank Account Details Penny-drop verification
08. Disbursal Funds credited in 10–15 mins post-approval

Proposed AI Intervention & Wireframe

Replace the intimidating static upload wall with a guided conversational AI assistant: verify one document at a time using edge vision heuristics to flag glare, crop, or low contrast in <15s synchronously instead of rejecting asynchronously hours later.

Primary North Star

First-Session KYC Completion (+24%)

Share of borrowers completing KYC cleanly in their initial session without dropping off.

Secondary Metric

Document Re-Upload Rate (-45%)

Eliminates post-submission rejections caught pre-flight on the user’s camera.

PART 02 • MARKETPLACE TRUST TEARDOWN

PhonePe: What Breaks When Lending Is a Marketplace, Not a Lender

When high-trust UPI users get routed into external NBFC webviews with disconnected repayment steps, conversion ruptures.

The Structural Trust Chasm

The borrower begins inside PhonePe — trusted every single day for payments — then gets abruptly dumped into an unbranded third-party lender webview asking them to re-type PAN and salary data they already provided. Meanwhile, Autopay mandate setup happens post-disbursal as an isolated second task.

The 8-Step Marketplace Funnel

01. Discover Opens “Loans” tab inside PhonePe (high initial trust)
02. Choose Offer Selects pre-approved NBFC offer (e.g., DMI Finance)
03. Partner Handoff [TRUST RUPTURE] Redirected to foreign third-party partner webview
04. Re-Enter Details [FRICTION] Forced to re-type PAN, employment & income fields
05. OTP-Based e-KYC & 06. E-Sign Aadhaar OTP verification & digital loan agreement
07. Disbursal Funds credited to borrower’s bank account
08. Decoupled Autopay Mandate Separate detached task post-disbursal → silent abandonment
Proposal 1 • Highest Leverage

Co-Branded Native In-App Bridge

Keep verification within PhonePe’s native trust shell. Pre-fill known user attributes (name, PAN, inferred income) via a context carry-over agent so users simply confirm instead of re-entering forms.

Proposal 2 • Collections Guard

Integrated Disbursal Autopay Mandate

Embed Autopay mandate authorization directly into the approval-to-disbursal gate (“One quick step to activate disbursal transfer”) instead of treating it as a disconnected post-loan chore.
