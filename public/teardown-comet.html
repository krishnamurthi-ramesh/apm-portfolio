Comet: From “Which Sneaker?” to “Which Comet Is Me?”

A product discovery teardown: reducing cognitive decision friction without stripping away the brand’s emotional sense of discovery.

Author: Krishnamurthi (AI PM)
Product Focus: Silhouette Finder 2.0
Prototype: Figma Site Prototype
Core Friction Hypothesis

“Users are provided technical footwear attributes, but the cognitive translation from attribute → personal lifestyle need → product choice creates high drop-off before checkout.”

Comet offers multiple silhouettes (X Lows, Aeres, Aeon v2, ASTRA, Apex) with comparison attributes. However, first-time shoppers struggle to translate these specs into personal fit and style, causing conversational amnesia and cart abandonment.

Solution: Silhouette Finder 2.0

A use-case-first discovery flow: asking 4 high-signal questions, then delivering ranked recommendations with clear reasoning.

Q1 • Use Case

What will you use them for?

Everyday commute, Long walks & travel, Night out, or Style-first statement.

Q2 • Fit Preference

How do you like your fit?

Roomy & relaxed, Balanced standard, or Snug locked-in athletic feel.

Q3 • Aesthetic Style

What is your style profile?

Minimalist clean, Streetwear bold, Retro court, or Chunky runner.

Q4 • Priority Vector

What matters most to you?

All-day underfoot comfort, Lightweight agility, Bold styling, or Universal versatility.

PM Judgment Call: Recommendation vs. Discovery

Balancing prescriptive AI guidance against organic brand serendipity.

Too much recommendation makes Comet feel like an impersonal, medical footwear catalog. Too little guidance creates paralyzing decision friction. The PM resolution: never recommend just one shoe immediately. Instead, surface 2–3 curated candidates and explain the distinct trade-offs between them:

1. X LOWS — Best for: Street + everyday wear, Roomier fit.
2. AERES — Best for: Lightweight everyday wear, Narrower streamlined fit.
3. APEX — Best for: Maximum cushioning, Relaxed casual weekend styling.

Success Metrics & A/B Experiment

Validating conversion lift without deteriorating return and exchange rates.

Primary North Star

Finder-Assisted Add-to-Cart

Conversion rate from Silhouette Finder completion to PDP add-to-cart within the same browsing session.

Secondary Lift

Zero-Repeat-Filter Sessions

Percentage of shoppers who reach checkout without manually resetting or retyping size/fit parameters.

Guardrail Metric

Return & Exchange Rate

30-day post-purchase return rate must not increase, confirming recommendation accuracy matches physical fit.
