KreditBee: The KYC Stack Nobody Fixed, Just Made Skippable

A single-lender app’s product decisions reveal exactly where borrowers give up — you just have to read the escape hatches they built.

Author: Krishnamurthi (AI PM)
Target Funnel: Consumer Digital Credit & Disbursal
Prototype: Figma Site Prototype
The Observational Friction Point

“The single most telling detail in KreditBee’s flow is that income verification was made explicitly optional and deferrable — an escape hatch acknowledging catastrophic mid-funnel drop-off.”

A first-time borrower has to produce, correctly frame, and photograph 3–4 separate documents on a phone in one sitting. Minor glare or edge crop causes asynchronous verification rejections hours later when borrower intent has permanently vanished.

The 8-Step Funnel Anatomy

Tracing the borrower journey from mobile sign-up to fund disbursal.

01. Download & Sign Up Mobile number + OTP verification
02. Eligibility Check PAN, DOB, monthly salary, PIN code (soft check, no docs)
03. KYC Document Upload [CHOKE POINT] Aadhaar/PAN, address proof, selfie — high drop-off
04. Income Verification [ESCAPE HATCH] Bank statement upload — explicitly made optional
05. Profile Verification Backend asynchronous OCR & underwriting review
06. Loan Offer Selection Amount + tenure selection with instant EMI breakdown
07. Bank Account Details Entered for penny-drop verification
08. Disbursal Funds transferred within 10–15 minutes once verified

The Proposed Solution & UX Wireframe

Replacing the static upload wall with instant client-side AI document guidance.

Replace the intimidating multi-document upload wall with a guided conversational AI flow: the agent asks for one document at a time, providing instant <15s client-side feedback if a photo is blurry, cropped, or reflective — before the user moves on, instead of issuing a rejection notification hours later.

Metrics That Prove It Worked

Directly tracking same-session completion and rejection elimination.

Primary Metric

First-Session KYC Completion Rate

Percentage of borrowers who start KYC and reach full submission within the very same app session without dropping to a later day (+24% target lift).

Secondary Metric

Document Re-Upload Rate

Frequency with which a document requires re-submission due to blur or crop. Falls by >45% because client-side pre-checks catch errors instantly.
