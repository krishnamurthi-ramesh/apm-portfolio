PhonePe: What Breaks When Lending Is a Marketplace, Not a Lender

Same consumer lending category as KreditBee, a different business model, and a fundamentally different failure mode — the external partner handoff break.

Author: Krishnamurthi (AI PM)
Model: Marketplace Brokerage vs. NBFC Partners
Figma: Community File
The Structural Trust Chasm

“The user starts inside PhonePe — an app trusted daily for UPI — then gets abruptly dumped into a partner lender’s webview in a foreign visual language, forcing them to re-decide: ‘Do I trust this?’”

This is not a document problem like KreditBee; it is an ecosystem trust continuity break. In addition, Autopay mandate setup is decoupled from loan approval, triggering severe post-disbursal drop-off and future collections overhead.

The 8-Step Marketplace Funnel

Tracing where trust and context fracture across external lender handoffs.

01. Discover Opens "Loans" inside PhonePe — high initial trust
02. Choose Offer Selects pre-approved loan from NBFC partners (e.g. DMI Finance)
03. Partner Handoff [CRITICAL FRICTION] Redirected to external partner application flow
04. Re-Enter Details [FRICTION] Forced to re-type PAN, employment, and income details
05. OTP-Based e-KYC Aadhaar OTP verification (smooth)
06. Review Offer & E-Sign Digital loan agreement executed
07. Disbursal Funds credited to bank account
08. Decoupled Autopay Setup Handed a separate second task — high silent drop-off

The Strategic Proposals & Figma Design

Carrying user context across partner boundaries and folding repayment into approval.

Proposal 1 • Highest Leverage

Co-Branded Native In-App Bridge

Keep the experience inside PhonePe’s native shell. An in-app agent carries known user details (verified name, PAN, and inferred income) across the hand-off — pre-filling and simply confirming rather than demanding manual re-entry.

Proposal 2 • Repayment Guard

Integrated Disbursal Autopay Mandate

Fold Autopay setup into the same approval flow, framed as the final step of disbursal (“You’re approved! One quick step to activate automatic transfer.”) instead of a disconnected post-disbursal chore.

Validation Metrics

Primary North Star

Secondary Metric
