The Edge AI Safety Problem

“Quantized Vision-Language Models deployed on edge manufacturing hardware experience catastrophic hallucination drift when factory visual distributions shift — threatening operator safety and QA yield.”

Full-precision models are too heavy and expensive for industrial edge nodes, while naive 4-bit/8-bit post-training quantization degrades out-of-distribution reliability. This research designed a two-tier GPU-student / CPU-teacher inference cascade bounded by formal conformal prediction guarantees.

The 3 Core Technical Contributions

Bridging formal verification mathematics with real-world edge hardware constraints.

01 • Drift Detection

ppDRE Density Estimator

Constructed a non-parametric Projected Penalized Density Ratio Estimator (ppDRE) that continuously tracks distribution shifts between edge camera inputs and nominal calibration datasets in real time.

02 • Two-Tier Cascade

Student / Teacher Cascade

Routine visual verification runs on an ultra-lightweight 4-bit student model on the local edge accelerator. If density drift or entropy exceeds safety bounds, the system automatically routes to the full-precision teacher.

03 • Mathematical Bounds

Conformal Safety Guarantee

Guarantees that the true physical defect classification is contained within the model’s predicted set with a user-specified probability (e.g., 95% or 99%), transforming black-box heuristic guesses into mathematically certified decisions.
